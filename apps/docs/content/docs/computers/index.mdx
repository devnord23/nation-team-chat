---
title: NATION Isolated PC
description: Help for your Nation Team Chat workspace.
---

An Isolated PC gives your agent a place to browse, work with files, and use tools. Availability is managed by your workspace owner.

Open the Computer panel to follow the available desktop and connection status. If it is unavailable, retry or contact NATION support.

A desktop connection and an agent reply are separate states: review the conversation and work record to confirm what completed.

[Contact NATION support](https://t.me/thenation_city).
