---
title: Self hosted composio
description: Help for your Nation Team Chat workspace.
---

Connected tools let agents work with services enabled for your workspace. Available connections depend on your workspace configuration.

Use the connection flow provided in the app. Keep passwords and secret keys out of chat. Review the task and requested permissions before authorizing work.

If a connection stops working, ask your workspace owner to check it in Admin.

[Contact NATION support](https://t.me/thenation_city).
