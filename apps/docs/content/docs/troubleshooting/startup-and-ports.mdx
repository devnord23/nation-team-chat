If a reply or desktop connection fails, check the conversation status and try again. Your saved agents and conversations remain available.

If an agent is still working, wait for its result or stop the turn before changing the task.

For repeated problems, contact NATION support with the action you tried and the time it happened. Do not include passwords, keys, or private files.

[Contact NATION support](https://t.me/thenation_city).
