---
title: Attachments and search
description: Help for your Nation Team Chat workspace.
---

Your agents and conversations stay in your workspace. Use a direct chat for one agent or a group for work involving several teammates.

Mention a teammate with `@` to ask for its reply. A message without a mention goes to the group’s default responder.

Follow activity in the conversation, inspect the result, and give feedback. Review requested approvals before work proceeds. Stop a turn from the conversation when you need to change direction.

[Contact NATION support](https://t.me/thenation_city).
