What stays fixed across all four

Materials and palette are shared on purpose, so the choice comes down to silhouette, head construction and the signature element. Whichever one you pick, the role family keeps about 75% of it: head shell, face glass, eye system and signature stay; props, posture, shell trims and expression tuning change per job.
